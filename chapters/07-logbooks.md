# 7. Logbooks

Status: outlined

## Job

Show the logbook as what a person writes down, because the archive and the alarm log will not remember it.

## Covers

- Three different memories from one shift. The archive holds values. The alarm log holds alarm state changes. The logbook holds the sentence a person wrote: what changed, what failed, and what the next shift must know.
- Olog, the logbook service Phoebus talks to. The Phoebus log client can also post to ELOG.
- ELOG, the electronic logbook from PSI, still in use as its own web logbook.
- Other logbooks only as the same job. Phoebus documents a pluggable logbook client, including an SNS operations logbook beside Olog and ELOG. The book names that plug and does not describe the service.
- "Send to logbook" from a screen or a plot: the display in chapter 4 can attach a picture. The text is still a person's entry.

## Stays out

- Log excerpts, ticket numbers, and how any one facility files its entries.
- A comparison of logbook database schemas.
