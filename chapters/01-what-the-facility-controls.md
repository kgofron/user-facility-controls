# 1. What the facility is controlling

Status: outlined

## Job

Tell the reader what a user facility is asking its control system to do, and where EPICS stops being the authority.

## Covers

- A shift: beam, instrument, sample, and the dataset the user takes home.
- Accelerator controls and beamline controls as neighboring systems. This book stays on the beamline side and treats the accelerator as a source of beam and timing.
- The safety boundary in one page: personnel safety, equipment protection, and software limits. Chapter 9 returns to the distinction.
- The instrument subject areas later chapters take one at a time: motion and sensors, then detectors.
- Why two facilities can both run EPICS and still feel different: a chopper spectrometer and a storage-ring beamline.
- One paragraph on XFELs and muon sources, then leave them.

## Stays out

- Accelerator physics.
- A tour of every subsystem. Later chapters own those.

- The two locked examples, once chosen. See [outline.md](../outline.md).
