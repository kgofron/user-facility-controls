# 6. The archive

Status: outlined

## Job

Show the archive as the memory of process-variable values. The Archiver Appliance is the system this chapter explains. The Channel Archiver is the older store a facility may still retrieve.

## Covers

- What gets archived is a choice. A policy decides the channels, the rate, and how the samples are reduced later. Channel Finder is one way a site makes that list.
- The EPICS Archiver Appliance: a clustered archiver aimed at millions of process variables, with short-term and long-term storage and retrieval from CS-Studio, including Phoebus Data Browser.
- The Channel Archiver, briefly. It is the older index-based archiver. The Appliance documents limited retrieval from existing Channel Archiver data sources. That is the handoff, not a second manual.
- How this differs from chapter 5. The alarm log records that an alarm was raised and acknowledged. The archive records the value.

## Stays out

- Appliance cluster layout, storage tuning, and retrieval MIME types.
- A Channel Archiver installation guide.
